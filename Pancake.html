American Pancakes

American Pancakes are a traditional breakfast staple known for their thick, fluffy, and spongy texture. They are best served warm, stacked high, and topped with a pat of butter,maple syrup, or fresh fruits like bananas and blueberries.

Ingredients

  • Flour: 1 ½ cups (all-purpose flour)
  • Baking powder: 3 ½ teaspoons (the secret to the fluffy texture)
  • Salt: 1 teaspoon
  • Sugar: 1 tablespoon
  • Milk: 1 ¼ cups
  • Egg: 1 large egg
  • Butter: 3 tablespoons (melted and slightly cooled)
  • Vanilla extract: 1 teaspoon (optional for extra flavor)

Preparation Method

Step 1: Mix the dry ingredients

In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, salt, and sugar until well combined.

Step 2: Mix the wet ingredients

In a separate bowl, beat the egg, then whisk in the milk, melted butter, and vanilla extract.

Step 3: Combine the mixtures

Make a well in the center of the dry ingredients and pour in the wet mixture. Stir gently with a spoon or a hand whisk just until the flour disappears. (Important note: Do not overmix; leaving some small lumps in the batter is perfectly fine and actually helps keep the pancakes soft and fluffy).

Step 4: Cook the pancakes

  1. Heat a non-stick skillet or griddle over medium heat and lightly coat it with a little butter or oil.
  2. Pour about ¼ cup of the batter onto the skillet for each pancake to form a circle.
  3. Cook until small bubbles form on the surface and the edges start to look dry (takes about 2 to 3 minutes).
  4. Flip the pancake gently and cook the other side for another 1 to 2 minutes until it turns golden brown.
